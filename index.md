## An aggregated catalogue of benchmark measurements for solar-like oscillators
On this webpage, I keep an updated catalogue of robust benchmark measurements for solar-like oscillating stars. This includes stars that have independent fundamental parameter measurements and individual oscillation mode frequencies. An example of this are eclipsing binaries containing a red giant oscillator. 

My aim with this catalogue is not to have a complete account of all benchmark stars, but rather, to keep a curated list of measurements that I am convinced are robust relative to their uncertainty.
It is my hope that this will be useful for anyone wishing to perform seismic modelling inference.


If you use this, thank you for your interest. For now, there is no single reference you can cite, rather, please state in your article that you used the \textsc{oscillating-bench} catalogue, and cite the individual references for the oscillator measurements you used in your paper.

### Eclipsing SB2 binaries
These have the mass and radius measured fully independently of the oscillations. This offers a way to directly benchmark asteroseismic predictions.

### SB2 binaries with a mass-constraint
For astrometric binaries, the inclination can be measured without eclipses. With radial velocities, this allows direct measurement of the stellar mass, but not the radius.

### Asteroseismic binaries
Asteroseismic binaries host two oscillators, which makes them a powerful tool for seismic comparison.
